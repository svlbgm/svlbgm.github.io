Ongoing research & coursework
How fragile are LLM evaluations?

In a team research project on Turkish LLM robustness, we explore how answer order, prompt format, and instruction language can change model rankings. The question: are we measuring capability, or sensitivity to the test?

Prediction is not the same as causation.

My causal inference coursework uses the METABRIC breast cancer dataset to study treatment effects through propensity scores, weighting, and survival analysis. This is an academic analysis, not clinical guidance.

Can emotion classifiers recognize uncertainty?

For a team text mining project using GoEmotions, we are considering how to evaluate when a classifier should abstain, alongside standard classification performance.
